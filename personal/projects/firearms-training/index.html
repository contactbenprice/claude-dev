What You'll Learn

Course Overview

This course is structured around three interlinked disciplines. You will leave with a deep, practical understanding of how firearms work, how projectiles behave, and how to handle both safely and responsibly.

🔩

Internal Ballistics

What happens inside the firearm from trigger pull to muzzle exit.

  • Combustion chamber physics
  • Propellant chemistry & burn rates
  • Pressure curves and barrel dynamics
  • Action types: semi-auto, bolt, revolver
  • Common malfunctions and their causes
📐

External Ballistics

The science of projectile flight from muzzle to target.

  • Trajectory and the effects of gravity
  • Drag, spin stabilization & precession
  • Wind deflection calculations
  • Ballistic coefficients (G1/G7)
  • Environmental factors: altitude, temperature, humidity
🎯

Terminal Ballistics

What occurs when a projectile interacts with its target.

  • Wound ballistics and tissue dynamics
  • Expansion, fragmentation & penetration
  • Ammunition selection principles
  • Material penetration and barrier performance
🛡️

Safe Handling & Mechanics

Foundational skills every responsible firearm owner must have.

  • The Four Fundamental Safety Rules
  • Loading, unloading, and administrative handling
  • Safe storage practices
  • Firearm anatomy and field-strip maintenance
  • Legal obligations for firearm owners
🏹

Range Practicum

Supervised live-fire sessions to apply everything you've learned.

  • Stance, grip, sight alignment & trigger control
  • Supported and unsupported shooting positions
  • Failure drills and stoppage clearing
  • Instructor-reviewed performance with feedback
📋

Course Completion

Leave with documented evidence of your training.

  • Certificate of completion (print & digital)
  • Personalized range performance summary
  • Resource packet: references, legal overview
  • Optional: written knowledge check

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to own a firearm to attend? +
No. Loaner firearms are available for the range practicum upon request and subject to prior approval. However, we strongly encourage you to use your own firearm if you own one, as training with the firearm you will actually use is far more beneficial. Contact us before enrolling to discuss loaner availability.
Is this course suitable for complete beginners? +
Absolutely. The course is designed from the ground up — no prior shooting experience is assumed. We cover foundational mechanics and safety before any live-fire exercises occur. Many of our students have zero prior experience and leave with a solid, confident foundation. Experienced shooters will gain depth in the ballistics science that most practical courses skip.
